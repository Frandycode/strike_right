Where will you sell?

What changes when you sell in more states

  • Selling online or shipping to customers in another state usually doesn't require registering your LLC there; an office, employees, regular in-person work or inventory there usually does.
  • States can require out-of-state sellers to collect sales tax once sales into the state pass a threshold (“economic nexus”).
  • Licensed professions and regulated products often need a license in each state where customers are.
